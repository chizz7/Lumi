Maya & the Tide-Pool Coins
Narrated by Willow · 4m 12s
0:004:12
Deep in the warm shallows, a small dolphin named Maya found three shiny coins resting in the sand. "Which one buys the biggest sea-berry?" they wondered. Just then, a wise old crab named Pip clicked over. "Let me show you something," Pip said, holding up the coins one by one. "This one is a penny — worth one. This one is a nickel — worth five. And this one…"

…Maya looked at all three coins. She thought about the big sea-berry she wanted. It cost ten. She had seven. Three more, she thought. If I save three more, I'll have enough.

The wise old crab smiled, just as he had when they counted coins together long ago. "Saving is how the best things happen," he said. "One coin at a time."
